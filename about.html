What FURLONG is

Sharp cards. Honest grades.

FURLONG is a small, high-conviction set of horse-racing plays posted every day — one Play of the Day plus a tight supporting card, each with a clear take price and the connections. Every card is graded against the result charts the next morning and the record is published here in full.

★

One card a day

No firehose. A handful of spots worth the ticket — the Play of the Day, plus Strong, Value and Score longshots, in post-time order.

✓

Graded in the open

Win, place, or beaten — every play is scored from the official payoffs and posted on the Track Record page. The bad days show too.

◆

Priced for value

Each play carries a “take” — the minimum price worth betting. We pass on odds-on chalk; the edge is in the number.
